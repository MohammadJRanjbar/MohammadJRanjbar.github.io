Currently

Researcher at the EPFL NLP Lab in Lausanne, working with Antoine Bosselut on multilingual audio understanding for Apertus 2, Switzerland's fully open multilingual LLM. Also a research assistant at the BIIC Lab at National Tsing Hua University in Taiwan, working with Chi-Chun Lee on affective speech foundation models across cultures.

I work on multilingual and low-resource NLP, mostly around speech. My research measures how far synthetic (TTS) speech can substitute for real data in low-resource languages, and builds the corpora and benchmarks that Persian NLP has been missing — including ParsVoice, the largest open Persian speech corpus (4,000+ hours), created for my M.Sc. thesis at the University of Tehran (GPA 4.0/4.0) under Azadeh Shakery and Heshaam Faili.

Hear my work بشنوید

Zero-shot voice cloning for Persian: give the model a few seconds of a voice it has never heard, plus a sentence — it speaks the sentence in that voice. This line was never recorded by a human — it's my thesis model, cloning a voice from the ParsVoice demo:

صدای خنده بچه‌ها در تمام کوچه پیچیده بود ▶ press to listen

"The sound of children's laughter echoed throughout the alley."

Full demo  ·  dataset (4,000+ hours)  ·  model  ·  code  ·  paper (EMNLP 2026)

That covers the generation side. On the understanding side, I built PARSA-Bench (Findings of EACL 2027) — 16 audio-language tasks for Persian, 10 of them new, from speech to classical poetry metre — and at EPFL I'm now bringing audio and text together inside one open multilingual LLM.
