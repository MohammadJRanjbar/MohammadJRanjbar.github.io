Mohammad Javad Ranjbar

محمدجواد رنجبر

I'm a researcher at the EPFL NLP Lab in Lausanne, working with Antoine Bosselut on multilingual audio understanding for Apertus 2, Switzerland's fully open multilingual LLM. I'm also a research assistant at the BIIC Lab at National Tsing Hua University, working with Chi-Chun Lee on affective speech foundation models across cultures.

My research is on multilingual and low-resource NLP, mostly around speech: how far synthetic speech can stand in for real data, and the corpora and benchmarks that Persian NLP has been missing. These include ParsVoice, the largest open Persian speech corpus, which I built for my M.Sc. thesis at the University of Tehran with Azadeh Shakery and Heshaam Faili.

Outside research, I love movies and games, and I like karaoke, long walks, exploring random places and visiting museums. You can see what I've been reading on Goodreads. A few of my favorite films and games are hidden around this site as easter eggs; see how many you can find.

I'm looking for PhD positions starting in 2027.

Selected publications full list

How Far Can Synthetic Speech Take Us? Synthetic Data Recipes for Pretraining Multilingual Speech Language Models
M. J. Ranjbar Kalahroodi*, A. K. Tarun*, C. I. Meister, A. Bosselut
Under review at ICLR 2027. *Equal contribution.

How far synthetic (TTS) speech can stand in for real speech when pretraining multilingual speech-language models, across six languages.

ParsVoice: A Large-Scale Multi-Speaker Persian Speech Corpus for Text-to-Speech Synthesis
M. J. Ranjbar Kalahroodi, H. Faili, A. Shakery
EMNLP 2026, main conference

The largest open Persian speech corpus: 4,000+ hours of audiobooks turned into multi-speaker TTS data by an automated pipeline, with a zero-shot voice-cloning model.

paper/ demo/ dataset/ code
PARSA-Bench: A Comprehensive Persian Audio-Language Model Benchmark
M. J. Ranjbar Kalahroodi*, M. Amini*, P. Bathayan, H. Faili, A. Shakery
Findings of EACL 2027. *Equal contribution.

The first Persian audio-language benchmark: 16 tasks and 7,645 samples, from speech recognition to the metre of classical poetry.

arXiv/ project page
PersianMedQA: Evaluating Large Language Models on a Persian-English Bilingual Medical Question Answering Benchmark
M. J. Ranjbar Kalahroodi, A. Sheikholselami, S. Karimi Arpanahi, S. Ranjbar Kalahroodi, H. Faili, A. Shakery
LREC 2026

20,785 expert-validated questions from 14 years of Iranian medical board exams, in Persian and English, for testing LLMs.

paper/ arXiv/ project page
PersianPunc: A Large-Scale Dataset and BERT-Based Approach for Persian Punctuation Restoration
M. J. Ranjbar Kalahroodi, H. Faili, A. Shakery
SilkRoadNLP workshop at EACL 2026

A 17M-sample dataset and a lightweight ParsBERT model for restoring punctuation in Persian text, such as speech-recognition output.

paper/ arXiv/ project page
