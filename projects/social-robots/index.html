See it work

The robot watches the person in front of it, recognizes their facial expression in real time, and responds with gestures, while taking spoken commands at the same time.

How it's built

The architecture is split into three layers, so each part can be swapped or improved on its own:

  • Layer 1
    Perception

    Camera and microphone input: faces and facial expressions are recognized in real time, and speech becomes commands.

  • Layer 2
    Decision

    Turns what the robot sees and hears into a response: which gesture to make, which command to carry out.

  • Layer 3
    Actuation

    Drives the robot's movements, so it answers with gestures people can read at a glance.

Why open source

  • The whole stack is designed to run on affordable hardware.
  • The code is public, so other labs and students can rebuild the robot and extend it.
  • It started as my B.Sc. thesis at Amirkabir University of Technology, advised by M. B. Menhaj and H. Taheri.
